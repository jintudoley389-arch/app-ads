Privacy Policy

Official Privacy Commitment

Learn how Jintu Tech Labs protects your data, upholds Play Store safety standards, and respects your privacy.

Effective Date: January 2026 | Last Updated: 2026

At Jintu Tech Labs (founded by Jintu Doley), accessible from this website and through our published Android applications, user privacy is one of our core priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we handle and how it is protected.

1. Information Collection & Usage

We do not collect, harvest, or store any sensitive personal data through this website. Any personal information you provide (such as your name, email address, or phone number) when contacting our support channels is used solely to respond to your technical questions, inquiries, and customer feedback.

2. Offline App Architecture & Local Data Storage

Our utility tools (including CaleSmart: EMI & Cash Counter) are engineered with an offline-first architecture. All calculation values, cash denomination records, and preferences remain completely on your local device. We do not transmit or store your financial figures on any external servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Monetization

Our website and mobile apps may use trusted third-party developer services:

  • Google Play Services: For secure app distribution and analytics.
  • Google AdMob: For monetization, which may use anonymized advertising identifiers compliant with Google's Play Store Family & Data Safety policies.
  • GitHub Pages: For secure web hosting and standard traffic logs (IP address, browser type).

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of user data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact information to third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information to us, please reach out so we can promptly delete it.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us directly at Support.jintutechlabs@gmail.com.
